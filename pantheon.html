Pantheon 🛡️

Who is Pantheon?

Pantheon is a fearless warrior from Mount Targon in League of Legends, known for his unbreakable will, spear combat, and relentless determination.

Lore

Once a mortal named Atreus, Pantheon sought power by climbing Mount Targon. He became the host of a celestial Aspect of War, gaining immense strength.

After the Aspect was destroyed, Atreus survived and continued fighting as a man, proving that human will alone can rival even the gods.

Pro Pantheon Plays 🛡️

Personality

Pantheon is determined, disciplined, and fearless. He represents resilience, showing that even ordinary humans can stand against overwhelming odds.

Abilities

  • Comet Spear: Throws or thrusts his spear, dealing damage to enemies.
  • Shield Vault: Leaps to a target enemy and stuns them.
  • Aegis Assault: Raises his shield to block damage from the front.
  • Grand Starfall: Leaps into the sky and crashes down at a location.
