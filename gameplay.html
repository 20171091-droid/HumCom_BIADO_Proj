Gameplay 🎮

League of Legends is a team-based strategy game where two teams of five players battle to destroy the enemy’s base called the Nexus. Each player controls a unique champion with special abilities.

🗺️ The Map (Summoner’s Rift)

The game is played on a map called Summoner’s Rift, which has three lanes: Top Lane, Mid Lane, and Bottom Lane, plus a jungle area filled with monsters.

⚔️ How the Game Works

  • Each team starts at their base.
  • Players earn gold by defeating minions, monsters, and enemies.
  • Gold is used to buy items and become stronger.
  • Destroy towers to reach the enemy base.
  • Destroy the Nexus to win the game.

👥 Roles in the Game

  • Top Laner – Strong solo fighter
  • Jungler – Roams and helps all lanes
  • Mid Laner – Magic damage dealer
  • ADC – Ranged damage carry
  • Support – Helps and protects teammates
