Vi 🥊

Who is Vi?

Vi is a tough and fearless fighter from Piltover/Zaun in League of Legends, known for her massive gauntlets and aggressive fighting style.

Lore

Vi grew up in the harsh streets of Zaun, learning to survive through strength and determination. She eventually became known for her powerful fists and bold attitude.

Later, she worked with Piltover as an enforcer, using her strength to fight crime. Despite her rough personality, she has a strong sense of justice and loyalty.

Personality

Vi is bold, confident, and straightforward. She doesn’t like to overthink things and prefers action, but deep down she cares about protecting others.

Abilities

  • Vault Breaker: Charges forward with a powerful punch that knocks back enemies.
  • Denting Blows: Every third hit deals bonus damage and reduces enemy armor.
  • Excessive Force: Empowers the next attack to deal damage in a cone.
  • Assault and Battery: Charges at a target enemy, knocking aside anyone in the way.
